# EVE Command Center v3.6.41

- Redesigned the embedded Industry production workspace with a compact shared header, consistent controls, full-width component selection, hover/pressed/focus states, readable component cards and dedicated Inputs, Blueprint & jobs, Stock & transfers, and Notes sections.
- Component inputs now open their own inspector. Select an owned blueprint by toon and source container to recalculate that component's material efficiency and inputs. Existing reservations, configured child work and job links are protected from destructive recalculation.
- Added explicit Match existing EVE job: inspect installer, blueprint instance, facility, runs, timestamps and status before linking. Links survive restart, reject conflicting/duplicate jobs, and never automatically create stock, consumption or delivery.
- Added named source filters for component blueprints and personal inventory, named production-location choices, custom container names, authorized structure/station names and missing type-name lookup through existing ESI infrastructure. Names are display metadata; reservation identities remain stable. Structure lookup may require reconnecting the toon for read-structures permission.
- Fixed project deletion with a visible Delete button and inline confirmation. Confirmed deletion releases that project's reservations atomically and retains a snapshot of the plan and released allocations in audit history; EVE assets and jobs are unchanged.
- Added explicit saved transfer-needed notes. A transfer requirement is not proof of completed hauling. Corporation inventory, full per-node quantity reconciliation, facility-aware math, bulk assignment, financial attribution and further Market phases remain pending.
- Expanded regression coverage for component blueprint selection, actual-job evidence and persistence, duplicate-job prevention, renamed-container reservations, structure verification and deletion with allocated stock. Rendered the workspace at desktop and compact sizes and verified full-row selection in WPF.
